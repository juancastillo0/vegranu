## Toasted Legumes (Garbanzo/Chickpea/Lentils/Black Beans/Peas)

<Gallery directory="baked-snacks"/>

### Instructions

- Cook the legume and leave to dry
- Prepare the sauce or spices to coat the legumes
- High fat like nut/seed butter and oil or high carb like date syrup will improve the texture of the final product. 
  - You could add sesame seeds or grain flakes for additional texture and presentation
- Before adding to the oven, you may accelerate drying by heating in a large pan and stiring constantly
- Add to a convection oven or air fryer and cook until toasted


## Hummus With Bread

Hummus and bread is a very nutritions combination that is easy to make. Toast the bread and spread the hummus, that's it.
Add kimchi for a very complete and balanced meal with grains, legumes, seeds and fermented vegetables.

## [Drinks, Shakes and Smoothies](./drinks-shakes-smoothies.md)

In some cases, just a tasty drink can blunt hunger. If you what to cut calories, opt for an infusion or spiced drink, tea, coffee with low calories.
However, [Shakes and Smoothies](./drinks-shakes-smoothies.md#shakes-and-smoothies) can also be great way to consume a lot of calories and nutrients very fast, which may be specially important if you exercise.
